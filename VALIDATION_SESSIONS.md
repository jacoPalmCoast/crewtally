# Checking CrewTally with real owners (before Phase 3)

Twenty minutes each with three or four people who pay day or hourly workers. Aim for a mix: a homeowner in the middle of a remodel, a small landlord, and a crew lead who pays helpers.

## Set-up
- Open the design canvas "CrewTally — iPhone screens" on your phone, in Play mode, starting at Sign in. Or show the images in `docs/screens/` in order.
- Say: "This is an early design, not a real app. You can't break anything. I'm testing the design, not you."
- Don't explain the screens. When they ask "what does this do?", say "what do you think it does?"

## Script
1. **Today, before the app** (3 min). "How do you keep track now of who worked and what you've paid? What goes wrong?"
2. **Record a day** (4 min). Show Today. "Marco worked a full day, Dee worked half a day, and Sam worked 8 hours. Record that."
3. **Payday** (4 min). "It's Friday. Pay everyone what they're owed. Luis gets a check, everyone else cash."
4. **Proof** (3 min). "Get proof that Marco got his cash." Hand them the phone for the hand-over screen.
5. **Send a record** (2 min). "Send Dee a receipt for what you paid her." Then ask: "Would you be OK with Dee seeing this?"
6. **What it cost** (2 min). "How much has the kitchen cost you in labor so far?"
7. **Wrap-up** (2 min). "What would stop you using this? What's missing? Would you pay for it, and how: monthly, once, or per project?"

## Deciding what to change
- **Two or more people stumble on the same step:** change the design before Phase 3.
- **One person stumbles:** note it and watch for it in the TestFlight pilot.
- **They use different words than the app** (for example "paid out" instead of "payment"): consider their words.
- Record their pricing answers as they said them. Don't round them into a decision.
